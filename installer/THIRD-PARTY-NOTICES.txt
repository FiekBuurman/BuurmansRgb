BuurmansRgb installs the following programs alongside itself. They are separate programs with
their own licences; BuurmansRgb talks to OpenRGB over its local SDK network protocol.

OpenRGB 1.1
  Licence: GNU General Public License v2.0
  Source:  https://codeberg.org/OpenRGB/OpenRGB (tag release_1.1)
  Website: https://openrgb.org

PawnIO 2.2.0
  Licence: freeware, see https://pawnio.eu
  Source:  https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO.Setup

The settings app (BuurmansRgb.UI.exe) contains these fonts. Their licence texts are in the
Fonts folder next to it.

Bricolage Grotesque (Regular, SemiBold, ExtraBold)

  Source:  https://github.com/ateliertriay/bricolage
